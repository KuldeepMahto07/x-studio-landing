Questions

Will it sound like a bot?

You set the voice and topics, and it learns from your past posts. Every post sits in a queue you can edit before it goes out.

Is it safe for my account?

It uses X's official API, posts original content at a normal human pace, and never follows, likes, or DMs on your behalf.

How is this different from Typefully or Tweet Hunter?

Those help you write and schedule. X Studio writes and posts for you, so you can stay consistent without spending time every day.
